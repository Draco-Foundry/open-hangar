See it in action

Screens show a fictional demo account.

Private by design

Runs entirely in your browser using the RSI session you're already signed in with. No password, no account, no server. Nothing leaves your device.

Your whole fleet

What your ships are worth today, what your CCUs saved you, your loaners, plus fleet stats and filters for everything. Search it all from one box.

Wishlist and store

Every ship's store price in your currency. Keep a wishlist and see what's on sale now, or find it in your buy-backs.

Org Fleet

Combine your org's ship lists into one fleet and see which roles you've got covered and which ones you're missing.

Referral tracking

Recruits, prospects, conversion stats, reward tiers and event bonuses on one page.

Built to build on

Export everything as one JSON file to back it up or feed your own tools. The code is public and fully auditable.

How it works

  1. Install the extension for your browser.
  2. Sign in to robertsspaceindustries.com as you normally would.
  3. Click the Open Hangar icon and hit Scan.
