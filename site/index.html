Take the Hangar Tour

Screens show a fictional demo account. No real whales were harmed.

Private by Design

Runs entirely in your browser using the RSI session you're already signed in with. No password, no account, no server. Nothing leaves your device. What happens in your hangar stays in your hangar.

Your Whole Fleet

What your ships are worth today, what your CCUs saved you, your loaners, plus fleet stats and filters for everything. Search it all from one box, JPEGs included.

Wishlist and Store

Every ship's store price in your currency. Keep a wishlist and see what's on sale now, or find it in your buy-backs. Your wallet has been warned.

Org Muster (org fleet)

Combine your org's ship lists into one fleet and see which roles you've got covered and which ones you're missing. Finally settle who's bringing the Hull C.

Wingman Tracker (referrals)

Recruits, prospects, conversion stats, reward tiers and event bonuses on one page. See who's still waiting in the lobby.

Built to Build On

Export everything as one JSON file to back it up or feed your own tools. The code is public and fully auditable. Your data, persisted like PES, minus the wipes.

Pre-Flight Checklist (how it works)

  1. Install the extension for your browser.
  2. Sign in to robertsspaceindustries.com as you normally would.
  3. Click the Open Hangar icon and hit Scan. Big fleet? Grab a coffee.
