# Roadmap

The player roadmap is at **openhangar.space/whats-next**.

The extension is the scraper: it reads your own RSI account (hangar, buy-backs, balances,
org, referrals) in your browser and keeps it locally, with optional sync to
openhangar.space. From 0.3.0 on, the extension gets reading improvements and fixes; new
features are built on the website.

Ideas and bug reports: GitHub issues or our Discord.
