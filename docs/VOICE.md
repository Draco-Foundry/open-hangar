# Open Hangar Voice

Everywhere Open Hangar talks to people (the site, the extension, emails), it
sounds like a fellow Citizen: fun, a little cheeky, full of 'verse flavour. The
action stays clear though: what happened, what to do next, and any warning must
be obvious at a glance. Legal and privacy pages stay plain. No em dashes; Title
Case headings. (#256)

## Rules

- Clear first, cheeky second: the plain meaning must survive the joke.
- One joke per message, max. Errors stay short.
- Never mock the user, their spending or their fleet size.
- Exact numbers, prices and warnings are never jokes.
- Fan project: no CIG logos, art or official slogans; keep the "not affiliated"
  line wherever we have a footer.
- No em dashes; Title Case headings.
